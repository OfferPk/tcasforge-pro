# TCASForge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

Airborne Collision Avoidance Systems (TCAS II v7.1 and ACAS X) are legally mandatory flight-safety avionics systems installed on over 45,000 commercial air transport and military cargo aircraft globally. Traditional transponder and TCAS bench test sets (Aeroflex/VIAVI IFR 6000, Tel-Instrument T-49CA, APM-424) cost \$30,000 to \$75,000 per hardware unit. TCASForge Pro provides an accessible, browser-native development, simulation, and protocol compliance verification workbench for airline avionics engineering teams, MRO safety technicians, simulator developers, and flight test organizations.
